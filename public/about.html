ABOUT DCURS

Building a culture of
research & inquiry.

Dhaka Central University Research Society is a student-centered platform for learning, collaboration and research.

OUR VISION

A community where questions become knowledge.

DCURS aims to encourage students from all seven campuses and different disciplines to explore research, develop practical skills and collaborate on meaningful projects.

The society will create opportunities for workshops, seminars, research projects, publications and peer learning.

Vision

To foster a vibrant student research culture grounded in curiosity, evidence and innovation.

Mission

To equip students with research knowledge, practical skills and collaborative opportunities.

Values

Integrity, curiosity, collaboration, academic rigor and responsible innovation.

ABOUT DCURS

Building a Culture of Research

Dhaka Central University Research Society is a student-driven platform dedicated to research, learning and innovation.

WHO WE ARE

A Research Community Built by Students

DCURS creates opportunities for students to develop research skills, collaborate on projects and contribute meaningful ideas.

Our goal is to transform curiosity into structured research and research into real-world impact.

01

Vision

To build a strong research culture where students can explore ideas and create knowledge.

02

Mission

To provide research education, collaboration opportunities and academic support.

03

Values

Curiosity, integrity, innovation and evidence-based thinking.

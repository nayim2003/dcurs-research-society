WHO WE ARE

Research without boundaries.

Dhaka Central University Research Society (DCURS) is a student-centered platform for developing research skills, encouraging academic inquiry and connecting students across the seven campuses.

We transform curiosity into structured investigation and ideas into meaningful research outcomes.

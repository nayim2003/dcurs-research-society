WHO WE ARE

A research culture built by students, for students.

Dhaka Central University Research Society (DCURS) is envisioned as a student-centered platform for developing research skills, encouraging academic inquiry, and connecting students across disciplines.

We aim to turn questions into structured investigations and ideas into meaningful research outcomes.

01

Learn

Build strong foundations in research methodology, statistics, academic writing and analytical thinking.

02

Research

Collaborate on interdisciplinary projects and develop practical research experience.

03

Impact

Share evidence-based ideas and contribute to meaningful academic and social conversations.
